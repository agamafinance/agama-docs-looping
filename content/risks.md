# Risks

Every position on Agama — a direct pool deposit, agUSD, or sagUSD — is a deposit whose return depends on real-world private credit and bond performance, plus the smart contracts that hold and route the capital. Read this page before depositing, minting, or staking.

## Smart contract risk

Agama is currently live on testnet. An audit of the Lending Pools, agUSD, and sagUSD contracts is in progress ahead of mainnet.

## Private credit / bonds risk

Each pool's return follows the performance of the real-world asset behind it — private credit (Pool A, Pool B) or bonds (Pool C). That underlying performance can vary.
